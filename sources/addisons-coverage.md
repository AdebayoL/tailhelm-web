# Addison's: what the registered sources cover

This is the Build Spec's Addison's care-rules table, checked against the tier 1 documents now in `register.json` (9 October 2026). Quotes are copied from the stored copies. Section numbers refer to the SPC unless stated otherwise.

Every source here is tier 1. There is no tier 2 source yet, because the AAHA document supplied is the AAHA website's executive summary, not the guidelines. It says itself that it "is not a substitute for reading the guidelines in their entirety". It is not registered. The full text is J Am Anim Hosp Assoc 2023; 59:113–135, DOI 10.5326/JAAHA-MS-7368.

| Rule in the Build Spec | What the sources say | Status |
|---|---|---|
| DOCP injection interval, "every 25 to 31 days" | Zycortal §3.9 gives no fixed window. It says Zycortal "is intended for long term administration at intervals and doses dependent upon individual response". The second injection comes "approximately 25 days after the first dose". In the trial the interval ranged from 20 to 99 days, with "the majority of dogs having a dosing interval between 20 and 46 days". | **No published window.** The interval belongs entirely in the prescribed layer. The pack format's required `windowDays` needs to become optional or be dropped. |
| Blood tests around day 10 and day 25 | §3.9: "measure the serum sodium/potassium ratio (Na⁺/K⁺ ratio) approximately 10 days after the first dose" and "At approximately 25 days after the first dose, re-evaluate the dog and measure the Na⁺/K⁺ ratio." When the interval is being lengthened: "Evaluate the electrolytes every 5–9 days". | **Covered**, but only for the start of treatment. The SPC sets no routine monitoring schedule after that, so later tests are set by the vet. |
| Na:K ratio as a derived value | §3.9 names the "serum sodium/potassium ratio (Na⁺/K⁺ ratio)". | **Covered** as a definition. The SPC's reference band appears only inside dose-adjustment instructions. Showing it to owners needs the vet's view, because it reads as a judgement. |
| Daily glucocorticoid | §3.9: "Zycortal replaces the mineralocorticoid hormones only. Dogs with combined glucocorticoid and mineralocorticoid deficiency should also receive a glucocorticoid such as prednisolone". Prednisolone SPC §4.5: "Treatment should not be withdrawn suddenly". | **Covered** that a glucocorticoid is part of the plan. How often it is given is prescribed. Nothing here backs "once a day". |
| Reminder 2 hours after the usual time | Not a clinical rule. It is product behaviour. | Needs no source. It should not be presented as a care rule. |
| Atypical Addison's (no DOCP) | No tier 1 source defines it. The AAHA summary describes it as "Group 4", meaning clinical signs without the classic electrolyte changes. | **Gap.** Needs the full AAHA guidelines. |
| Fludrocortisone | The SmPC §4.1 covers people: "partial replacement therapy for primary adrenocortical insufficiency in Addison's disease". There is no UK veterinary product. | **Prescribed layer only.** An owner can add it as a daily medicine their vet prescribed. The pack makes no rule about it. |
| Stress plan | §3.9: "Prior to a stressful situation, consider temporarily increasing the dose of glucocorticoid." | **Covered** for prompting the owner to agree a stress plan with their vet, and for showing the vet's typed instructions. This sentence is addressed to vets, so it is never shown to owners as advice. |
| Crisis signs and "Call your vet now" | §3.4 describes Addisonian crisis in clinical terms only: "severe hypovolaemia, dehydration, pre-renal azotaemia and inadequate tissue perfusion". §3.9 lists "depression, lethargy, vomiting, diarrhoea or weakness" as signs that need the plan changing. | **Partial.** The §3.9 signs can back "mention this to your vet". An owner-facing emergency list (collapse, sudden weakness) needs the full AAHA guidelines. |
| Opened Zycortal vial, "use within 120 days" | §5.2: "Shelf life after first opening the immediate packaging: 4 months." The label says: "Once opened use within 4 months." §5.3: "Do not store above 30 °C. Do not freeze." | **Covered, with a correction.** It is 4 calendar months, not 120 days. The discard-by date is the opening date plus 4 months. |
| Missed or late injection | Nothing in the SPCs. | **Gap** for any published rule. An overdue banner against the vet's own date, with "contact your vet", needs no published rule. |
| Side effects worth logging | §3.6 lists "Polydipsia" and "Polyuria" as very common, and "Lethargy, Decreased appetite … Vomiting, Diarrhoea" among the common ones. | **Covered** as observation types the owner can log. |
| Other medicines | §3.8: "Use with caution when administering Zycortal concurrently with medicinal products which affect either serum sodium or potassium concentrations". Prednisolone §4.8 covers NSAIDs. | **Covered** for a prompt to tell the vet about any other medicine. |
| Heat and cold watch | Nothing. | **No rule.** It is out of launch scope anyway. |
| Happening now thresholds for Addison's | Nothing. | **No rule** unless the full AAHA guidelines give one. |
| Trend thresholds | Nothing. | **No rule** unless the full AAHA guidelines give one. |

## Supplied but not registered

- **AAHA executive summary.** It is a secondary summary, as explained above.
- **Fludrocortisone patient leaflet.** It is a human patient leaflet, and the SmPC is the registered document.
- **Post-authorisation assessment records.** These are the change history of the products. They hold no clinical content.
- **Duplicate prednisolone SPCs** under Vm 61300/5003 and 5004. The text is identical to 3003 and 3004, and this is noted on those entries.
